BUYER GUIDE

OEM pet car seat covers: a buyer’s specification checklist.

A buyer checklist for OEM pet car seat covers covering dimensions, fabrics, non-slip layers, attachment, cleaning, branding and packaging.

View manufacturer programRequest a quotation →
01

1. Define the vehicle fit

Specify the vehicle seat or cargo area, coverage, access openings, seat-belt compatibility and whether the format is a bench cover, hammock, extender or cargo liner. Include dimensions and photos or a simple drawing when fit is critical.

02

2. Confirm the fabric system

Document face fabric, backing, padding, mesh, waterproofing expectations and non-slip layers for the intended cleaning, comfort and durability requirements. Ask which materials can be color-matched or substituted.

03

3. Detail attachment and safety

List headrest straps, seat anchors, elastic, buckles, openings and reinforcement points. Confirm that the attachment method stays stable during normal vehicle use and does not block seat-belt access.

04

4. Define cleaning requirements

State whether the cover needs wipe-clean, machine-washable or removable components. Confirm seam construction, coating expectations, drying instructions and how the packed product should be presented.

05

5. Approve branding and packaging

Document logo method, label position, colorways, folded dimensions, individual packaging, inserts, barcode requirements and carton markings. Packaging should protect the cover without excessive volume.

06

6. Prepare the sampling brief

Send the target dimensions, reference photos, quantity, market, material preference, logo artwork and packaging direction together. Confirm sample charge, lead time, revision rounds and the final approval standard before production.

Final MOQ, pricing, materials, lead time and packaging depend on the approved product specification and order quantity.
