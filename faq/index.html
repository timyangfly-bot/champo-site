BUYER FAQ

Common sourcing questions.

Key information for buyers evaluating a trial order, custom development or repeat production.

What products does CHAMPO manufacture?

We focus on car organizers, automotive pet protection products, cargo liners and vehicle tool bags.

Can I start with a trial order?

Yes. Practical trial quantities are available, while the final MOQ depends on material, construction, branding and packaging.

How long does sampling take?

A typical sample takes about seven days after specifications and materials are confirmed.

What is the usual production lead time?

Peak-season production lead time is approximately 30 days. The confirmed schedule depends on quantity, material availability and customization.

What can be customized?

Materials, dimensions, functional details, colors, logo application, labels, inserts and retail packaging can be developed for OEM and private-label programs.

What certifications are available?

Current ISO 9001, GRS and amfori BSCI documents are available on the website for buyer review. Scope and validity should be checked for each program.
