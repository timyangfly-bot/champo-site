BUYER FAQ

Before you request a quote.

Can you customize the organizer structure?

Yes. Dimensions, compartment layout, handles, closures, support panels and functional details can be adjusted after technical review.

Can we add our logo and retail packaging?

Logo application, labels, colorways, inserts and retail-ready packaging can be included in private-label development.

How should buyers request a quote?

Provide a reference style, required size, material, quantity, target market, logo method and packing requirements.
