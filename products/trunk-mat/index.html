Trunk Mat

A trunk mat is a protective liner designed to shield vehicle cargo areas from spills, dirt, scratches, and wear.

It helps maintain the resale value of vehicles by keeping trunk surfaces clean and damage-free.

Key Features

  • Custom-fit for specific vehicle models
  • Waterproof and stain-resistant materials
  • Deep ridges to contain spills and liquids
  • Non-slip backing for secure placement
  • Easy to remove and clean
  • Durable construction for long-lasting protection
