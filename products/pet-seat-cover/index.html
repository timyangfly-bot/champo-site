Pet Seat Cover

A pet seat cover is an essential car accessory designed to protect vehicle seats from pet hair, scratches, dirt, and moisture.

It helps maintain a clean car interior while providing a comfortable and safe space for pets during travel.

Key Features

  • Waterproof and scratch-resistant materials
  • Easy installation with elastic straps or buckles
  • Machine washable for easy cleaning
  • Non-slip backing to prevent shifting
  • Available in multiple sizes for different vehicles
  • Breathable mesh designs for pet comfort
