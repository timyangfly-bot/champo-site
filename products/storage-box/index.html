Storage Box

A car storage box is a practical trunk organizer designed to keep vehicle cargo areas tidy and organized.

It helps maximize trunk space efficiency while protecting items from shifting during travel.

Key Features

  • Collapsible design for easy storage when not in use
  • Heavy-duty waterproof materials
  • Multiple compartments for better organization
  • Non-slip bottom to prevent sliding
  • Easy to clean with wipeable surfaces
  • Reinforced handles for comfortable carrying
