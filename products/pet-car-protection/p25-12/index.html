03 / TWO SUPPORT INSERT OPTIONS

Match the insert to the use case.

Choose between MDF and hollow plastic board to balance support, product weight and care requirements.

OPTION A

MDF insert

  • Higher rigidity in the previously compared same-thickness samples.
  • Heavier construction.
  • Suitable when firmness and material cost are priorities.
  • Keep dry; do not wash the board.
OPTION B

Hollow plastic board

  • Lighter construction.
  • Water-resistant plastic insert that can be rinsed.
  • Suitable when lower product weight and easier insert cleaning are priorities.
  • Support depends on the selected board and finished configuration.

This comparison follows the supplied material information. Cleaning guidance for the removable board does not imply that the complete cover is machine washable.

Which support board should I choose?
