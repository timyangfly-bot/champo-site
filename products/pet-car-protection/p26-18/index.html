BUYER QUESTIONS

P26-18 specifications and ordering

What are the P26-18 product and folded dimensions?

The product measures 168 × 137 cm. Folded dimensions are 62 × 43 × 12 cm, and the unit weight is 2.9 kg.

How much wider is the P26-18 base?

The base is 63 cm wide compared with a 45 cm standard reference, adding 18 cm. At the same length, this is approximately 40% more base area.

What supports the base and protective panels?

The widened base uses a 5 mm hollow board. Removable plastic support rods help support the protective panels.

Can the inner liner be removed for washing?

Yes. The inner liner is removable and washable. Follow the care instructions confirmed for the approved production sample.

Will P26-18 fit my vehicle?

Fit depends on the rear-seat width, seatback and headrest layout. Compare your vehicle measurements with the product dimensions and confirm fit using an approved sample before ordering.

Are all layers in the material illustration confirmed?

The supplied layered-material image is a reference illustration. Confirm the final material composition and layer order against the approved production sample before purchase.
