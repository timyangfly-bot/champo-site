Seat Hanging Bag

A seat hanging bag is a practical car seat organizer designed to provide extra storage on the back of a vehicle seat.

It helps keep the car interior clean and organized while protecting the seat back from dirt, scratches and shoe marks caused by passengers or children.

Key Features

  • Multi-pocket storage design for better organization
  • Protects seat back from dirt, scratches and shoe marks
  • Durable Oxford fabric or PU leather material
  • Easy installation with adjustable straps
  • Suitable for most cars, SUVs and family vehicles
